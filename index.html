Philosophy

European strategy, local execution

I have spent most of my career connecting European strategy with local market execution — campaign development, go-to-market communication, retail and trade marketing, and coordination between European markets and global HQ in Japan.

In practice that means adapting a centrally built message so it still makes sense in each country, steering agencies, running retail and partner activity, owning the P&L behind it, keeping 20+ markets moving in the right direction — and plenty of other, equally fascinating work.
